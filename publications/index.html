PUBLICATION POLICY

Publication status is reported by evidence stage

Adaptive Movement Research distinguishes between a manuscript in development, a released preprint, a submitted manuscript and a formally peer-reviewed publication.

A preprint establishes a public scholarly record but does not constitute peer-reviewed acceptance.

Journal submission and editorial evaluation are recorded as publication-process events rather than evidence of scientific validation.

Future publication records will be added only when the corresponding scholarly stage has actually been reached.

CURRENT PROGRAMME

Beyond the foundational publication

Adaptive Movement Research has progressed beyond the initial conceptual Framework Paper into operational and experimental development.

The current programme examines biological state, State-Dependent Biological Load, multisystem regulation, recovery and adaptation while developing a first feasibility design for empirical testing.

These developments are recorded as current research architecture and should not be interpreted as findings established by the released preprint.
