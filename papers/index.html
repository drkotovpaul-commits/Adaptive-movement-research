RESEARCH PROGRAM

From conceptual framework to empirical research

The Adaptive Movement Research program investigates whether measurable properties of human movement can provide useful information about biological adaptation at the systems level.

The research treats movement quality as a potentially integrative observable variable that may reflect the interaction of multiple regulatory, mechanical, sensory and adaptive processes operating across the organism.

The program therefore develops a structured framework for describing movement characteristics, identifying measurable variables and establishing a pathway toward empirical validation.

PAPER 01

Movement quality as a systems-level indicator

The first manuscript establishes the conceptual foundation of the research program and defines movement quality as a potentially informative systems-level observable.

Subsequent stages will address operational definitions, measurement methodology, empirical validation and interpretation of observed movement patterns.
