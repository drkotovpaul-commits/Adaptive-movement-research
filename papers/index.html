CURRENT MANUSCRIPT

Paper 1

Beyond Homeostasis and Allostasis: Is Continuous Whole-Organism Coordination an Underdefined Biological Phenomenon?

A conceptual investigation examining whether current biological terminology explicitly defines continuous whole-organism coordination as an independent conceptual entity, or whether the relevant explanatory content remains distributed across several established concepts.

The manuscript is conceptual rather than experimental. It does not claim to establish the biological existence or absence of a new physiological process. Its purpose is to identify and characterize a potentially underdefined area suitable for subsequent empirical investigation.

RESEARCH POSITION

A conceptual foundation for empirical research

Paper 1 is deliberately positioned as a conceptual study rather than as a claim of a new physiological mechanism.

Its intended contribution is to determine whether an identifiable conceptual space exists between established descriptions of organismal structure, regulation, information, signaling, and communication.

Any subsequent empirical investigation will therefore be developed only after the conceptual and bibliographic foundations have been sufficiently established.
