Movement quality and biological adaptation

Adaptive Movement Research

INTRODUCTION

Research context

Human movement represents the observable result of coordinated activity across multiple biological and mechanical systems.

Changes in movement characteristics may therefore contain information about the functional state and adaptation of these interacting systems.

The present research program examines movement quality from this systems-level perspective. Rather than restricting analysis to a single physiological, anatomical or mechanical variable, the research considers movement as an integrated observable outcome.

Particular attention is given to measurable properties such as consistency, variability, coordination and changes in movement characteristics under different conditions.

The purpose of this manuscript is to establish a conceptual and methodological foundation from which these movement characteristics can subsequently be investigated empirically.
