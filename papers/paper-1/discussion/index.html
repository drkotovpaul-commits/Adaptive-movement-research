Adaptive Movement Research

DISCUSSION

Interpretation

This section will interpret the findings presented in the Results section within the conceptual framework established by the research.

Particular attention will be given to the relationship between observed movement characteristics and the proposed systems-level interpretation of biological adaptation.

The discussion will also consider alternative explanations, methodological limitations and the extent to which the observed findings support the central research premise.

Further implications for future research and development of movement-based indicators will be addressed.
