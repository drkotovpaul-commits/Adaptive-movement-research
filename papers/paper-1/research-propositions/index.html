FRAMEWORK PAPER · SECTION 4

Research Propositions and Falsifiability Criteria

From measurable relationships to sequential empirical testing

From Movement Organization to Whole-Organism Adaptation: An Integrated Framework for Multisystem Physiological Research

RESEARCH PROPOSITION

The framework generates testable relationships rather than predetermined conclusions

The purpose of the present section is to translate the conceptual and methodological structure established in the preceding sections into propositions that can be examined through empirical research.

These propositions concern relationships among movement organization, multisystem physiological response, recovery, repeated exposure and subsequent adaptation. They are not treated as established biological facts.

Each proposition therefore remains conditional on the successful operationalization, measurement and validation of the relevant variables.

The framework is structured so that unsupported relationships can be rejected rather than being protected by retrospective interpretation.

PROPOSITION 06 · DOWNSTREAM TEST
TESTING LOGIC

Downstream propositions depend on upstream evidence

Measurement first

A proposition concerning biological relevance cannot be evaluated adequately until the underlying movement characteristics have been defined, measured and shown to possess sufficient reproducibility.

The framework therefore treats feasibility, reproducibility and condition sensitivity as prior empirical requirements.

Relationship second

Relationships between movement organization and multisystem response are considered only after the corresponding movement measures have passed the preceding methodological stages.

This ordering reduces the risk that downstream biological interpretation is imposed on unstable or poorly defined movement measurements.

FALSIFIABILITY

The framework must permit contradictory findings

A candidate movement measure should not be retained solely because it is compatible with the conceptual framework. Its usefulness depends on whether its predicted relationships survive empirical testing.

Failure to demonstrate reproducibility, condition sensitivity or a predicted relationship with multisystem response must therefore remain a possible outcome.

Unsupported propositions may consequently be rejected, revised or replaced rather than being treated as confirmation of the framework.

This requirement is central to maintaining a testable research structure in which empirical findings can meaningfully constrain subsequent interpretation.

FALSIFICATION CRITERIA

Conditions under which propositions may be rejected

01

Insufficient feasibility

The candidate characteristic cannot be defined or measured consistently under the intended conditions.

REJECTION CRITERION
02

Insufficient reproducibility

Repeated observations fail to demonstrate a sufficiently stable and interpretable measurement structure.

REJECTION CRITERION
03

No condition sensitivity

A candidate characteristic does not respond in the predicted manner to defined experimental or contextual changes.

REJECTION CRITERION
04

No multisystem relationship

A validated movement characteristic fails to demonstrate the predicted relationship with the prespecified multisystem response.

REJECTION CRITERION
05

No reproducible repeated-exposure effect

Movement or response characteristics do not demonstrate reproducible change across repeated exposure when such change is predicted.

REJECTION CRITERION
06

Unsupported adaptive interpretation

Broader claims concerning adaptation remain unsupported when the preceding measurement and response relationships are not independently established.

STAGE 06
SCIENTIFIC POSITION

A testable framework rather than an established biological claim

The propositions presented here define a research structure for empirical investigation. They do not establish that movement characteristics are already validated indicators of biological adaptation.

The scientific contribution of the framework therefore depends on whether the proposed relationships can be operationalized, measured reproducibly and evaluated against observations that could contradict them.

Downstream relevance to adaptation remains contingent upon evidence generated through these staged empirical tests.

SECTION BOUNDARIES

What Section 4 establishes — and what remains empirical

This section establishes a sequence of research propositions and explicit criteria by which those propositions can be supported, rejected or revised.

It does not specify a single population, instrument, movement task, statistical model or biological endpoint that must be used in every future study.

Those decisions remain dependent on the empirical research question and the corresponding study design.

The proposed structure therefore functions as a falsifiable research programme rather than as a completed empirical validation.
