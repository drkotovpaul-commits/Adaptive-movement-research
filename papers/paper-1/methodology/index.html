METHODOLOGY

Methodological approach

This section will define the methodological approach used to investigate movement quality as a measurable systems-level variable.

The final manuscript will specify the study design, measurement procedures, variables, analytical methods and criteria used for interpretation.

Particular attention will be given to the reproducibility of measurements and to the distinction between observable movement characteristics and their biological interpretation.
