PAPER 01 · MANUSCRIPT

Movement quality as a systems-level indicator

A conceptual framework for investigating human biological adaptation through movement

Adaptive Movement Research

PAPER INFORMATION

Research manuscript

This manuscript investigates whether measurable properties of human movement can provide useful information about biological adaptation at the systems level.

The paper develops the conceptual foundation for treating movement quality as an integrative observable variable rather than as an isolated characteristic of a single anatomical or physiological system.
