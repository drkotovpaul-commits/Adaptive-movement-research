Paper 1

Conceptual Audit

This conceptual study examines whether established biological terminology explicitly represents continuous whole-organism coordination as a distinct organizational phenomenon.

The analysis does not propose a new biological theory and does not challenge the validity of established physiological concepts. Instead, it examines how existing concepts describe structure, regulation, communication, and organism-wide coordination.
