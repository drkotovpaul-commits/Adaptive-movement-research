FRAMEWORK PAPER

Conceptual and methodological foundation

This framework paper establishes the conceptual and methodological structure for investigating movement organization in relation to multisystem physiological response, recovery, repeated exposure and adaptation.

The paper distinguishes observable movement characteristics from biological interpretation and defines a staged structure through which proposed relationships can be evaluated empirically.

SCIENTIFIC POSITION

A research framework rather than an established biological claim

The framework defines a structure in which the relationship between movement organization, multisystem physiological response, recovery, repeated exposure and adaptation can be examined empirically.

It does not treat movement observations as substitutes for established physiological measurements, diagnostic procedures or biological mechanisms.

Downstream claims remain contingent upon empirical testing of the preceding measurement and response relationships.
