RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

Conceptual framework

This section defines the conceptual framework used to examine movement quality as a systems-level observable.

The framework will establish the relationship between measurable movement characteristics and the biological processes that may contribute to observed adaptation.

The final manuscript will specify the principal variables, their proposed relationships and the assumptions underlying their interpretation.
