INDEPENDENT RESEARCH PROGRAMME

Adaptive Movement Research

Movement organization, multisystem physiology, and whole-organism adaptation.

An independent research programme investigating how the organization of movement relates to dynamic multisystem physiological response, recovery, repeated exposure and adaptation, with long-term relevance to noncommunicable disease prevention.

Explore the Research Framework Paper

CENTRAL RESEARCH QUESTION

How does the organization of movement relate to multisystem physiological response and whole-organism adaptation?

Movement is treated here as both an observable expression of ongoing biological organization and a recurrent perturbation of the organism.

The programme investigates whether measurable characteristics of movement organization can be related, in time, to multisystem physiological response, recovery and subsequent adaptation without reducing the phenomenon to a single organ system or movement modality.

RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

RESPONSE LEVEL
03

Recovery and adaptation

EMPIRICAL TARGET

RESEARCH POSITION

A framework, not a new medical specialty

Adaptive Movement Research integrates perspectives from physiology, systems biology, Network Physiology, movement science and preventive medicine.

The programme does not claim a new biological law, diagnostic instrument or preventive intervention. Its purpose is to define testable relationships that can later be evaluated empirically.
